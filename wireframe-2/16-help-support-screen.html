Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new course?
Go to the Courses tab and tap the "Add Course" button. Fill in the course details and upload your study materials.
How does the AI scheduling work?
Our AI analyzes your course content and creates optimal study sessions based on your available time and learning preferences.
Can I customize my study schedule?
Yes! You can adjust session lengths, change study times, and modify the AI-generated schedule to fit your needs.
How do I track my progress?
Visit the Progress tab to see your completion rates, study streaks, and detailed analytics for each course.
