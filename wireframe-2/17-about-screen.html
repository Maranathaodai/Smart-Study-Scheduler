About Smart Study Scheduler

Smart Study Scheduler is an AI-powered study planning app that helps students optimize their learning through intelligent scheduling, progress tracking, and personalized study recommendations. Our app uses advanced algorithms to create the most effective study schedules based on your courses, available time, and learning preferences.

Features

• AI-powered study scheduling
• Progress tracking and analytics
• Course management
• Goal setting and achievement tracking
• Study tips and motivation
• Calendar integration
• Offline study support
